A dense one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
**Wednesday 18 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image: **41 stories**
across 7 regions, a UTC timeline strip, and an explicit date-check line.

The leads are Iran's strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain after the US hit a
missile site on Qeshm Island, Ukraine hitting the Kerch Bridge for a
second day — a road span collapsed into the strait — Xi meeting Putin in
Shenzhen at APEC a day before their three-way summit with Trump, and the
Quad meeting at leaders' level in Manila. Around them: Peltola winning
Alaska and a 56–44 Democratic Senate majority, Kahana elected Knesset
speaker, the EU proposing a €45bn loan for Ukraine, Voyager 1 reaching
one light-day from Earth, Nvidia's record $115.8bn quarter, OpenAI
launching GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, the US losing measles elimination status
after 26 years, Ebola passing 11,000 cases in DR Congo, COP31 deadlocked
in Antalya, and 8,000+ bomb threats across Europe in a month. Every item
was checked to have happened or been first reported on 18 November 2026
(UTC); nothing from an earlier day is presented as the 18th's news.

Every word on the page is drawn programmatically, so the text is crisp and
exact; the four illustration panels are AI-generated photographs (no text
in them) laid into the composition. The finished page was checked at full
size and at 300 px across for legibility, spelling and overlaps.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. It needs Node 22, no network,
no dependencies, and writes identical bytes on every run.
